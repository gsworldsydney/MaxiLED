Pergola LED Dimension System & Engineering Principles

F1 : Outer Edge Margin

Distance from the outermost bar ends to the first and last LED modules. Default: 500mm.

F2 : Full-Span Uniform Pitch (Δ)

Net spacing between LEDs = (Total Span - 2×F1 - Total LED Length) ÷ (N - 1). Maintains uniform visual pitch across joints.

Joint Step-Over (F3 & F4)

Preceding bar last LED to Joint (F3), and Joint to next bar first LED (F4). F3 + 50 + F4 = F2 always holds.

Rafter & Middle Bar Math

Derives roof slope angle (θ) and Rafter length via trigonometry. Auto-divides by 500mm (θ ≥ 6°) or 520mm (θ < 6°).
